✦ General ✦
CYBERTYPER is a modern neon space-themed browser arcade dedicated to making typing training genuinely fun. Engineered with pure client-side web standards, it features benchmark speed testing, arcade word-defense games, real-time racing vs AI bots, and orbital asteroid demolition with dynamic sound effects.
Yes! All our free online typing games are 100% free. You do not need to create an account or sign up to play.
All modern desktop and mobile browsers supporting modern HTML5 canvas and Web Audio are fully supported (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, Brave, and Opera) on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.
✦ Games & Mechanics ✦
WPM stands for Words Per Minute. Our WPM test tracks exactly how many words you can type correctly in one minute. It’s a great way to measure your progress!
The best way to improve typing speed is daily practice. We recommend starting with our online typing test to check your current speed, and then playing our arcade typing games every day to get faster.
Yes! Games like our falling words typing game and asteroid defense force you to focus on the right keys, which naturally improves your typing accuracy over time.
Typing industry standards define a standard word as exactly 5 keystrokes (including letters, spaces, and punctuation). Your live WPM is calculated as (Characters Typed / 5) / (Elapsed Minutes). Accuracy represents (Correct Characters / Total Keystrokes) * 100%.
Words drop from orbit toward your neon shield line. Typing the initial letter locks onto the word, and typing remaining letters vaporizes it. You start with 3 shields (lives). If a word hits the bottom barrier, you lose a shield. Losing all 3 shields ends the game.
You race side-by-side along an animated track against an autonomous AI competitor clocked at a steady 40 WPM pace. Typing accurately accelerates your racecar across the finish line to claim top spots on the local leaderboard.
In Falling Asteroids, asteroid size is directly proportional to word length:
  • Short words (2-4 letters): Small, rapid meteors.
  • Medium words (5-7 letters): Mid-sized balanced rocks.
  • Long words (8+ letters): Giant, high-threat asteroids that award massive score multipliers upon destruction.
There are zero shields in Falling Asteroids. If any asteroid breaches your perimeter or collides with your ship, it results in instantaneous catastrophic hull destruction, screen shake, and Game Over.
✦ Scores and Data ✦
All records, high scores, and leaderboard rankings are saved locally in your browser's private localStorage database under your browser profile.
Since scores are stored client-side in localStorage, they will reset if you:
  • Clear your browser's cookies or site data;
  • Switch to an Incognito / Private browsing window;
  • Play from a different web browser or different device.
No. CYBERTYPER does not collect, harvest, track, or sell any personal information. All gameplay state remains isolated on your device.
✦ Technical & Troubleshooting ✦
1. Check the speaker icon in the top header (make sure it shows 🔊, not 🔇).
2. Modern web browsers block audio autoplay until the user interacts with the page. Simply click anywhere on the page or press any key to unlock the Web Audio synthesizer.
CYBERTYPER uses hardware-accelerated canvas rendering. If experiencing frame drops, ensure "Hardware Acceleration" is turned ON in your browser settings, close unnecessary background tabs, or check if battery-saver mode is throttling your GPU.
