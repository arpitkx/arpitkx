TACTICAL COMBAT MANUAL

How To Play Falling Asteroids

Comprehensive combat rules, lock-on targeting mechanics, and survival strategies.

01

Acquire & Target Lock

Screen par descend hone wale kisi bhi asteroid ka pehla letter type karein. Ship ka turret automatic us asteroid par aim karke target lock kar leta hai aur glowing plasma tracer fire karta hai.

02

Plasma Fire Sequence

Word ke baaki bache letters ko sequence me accurately type karein. Har correct keypress par plasma beam hit karti hai aur word ke letters green highlight ho jaate hain.

03

Unlock & Emergency Retarget

Agar aapne galat asteroid lock kar liya hai aur doosra meteor ship ke bohot kareeb aa chuka hai, toh Backspace press karein. Target foran release ho jayega aur aap closest threat ko lock kar payenge.

04

Zero Shield Rule (Instant Kill)

Is defense mode me ship ke paas extra shields ya lives nahi hain! Agar koi bhi asteroid bottom perimeter line cross kar leta hai, toh interceptor direct explode ho jayega aur mission fail hoga.

⚡ Sector Waves & Scoring Multipliers

  • Score Formulation: Points asteroid ki word length aur current sector level par depend karte hain: Points = Word Length × 25 × Level.
  • Sector Upgrades: Har 8 asteroids destroy karne par next sector unlock hota hai. Har naye sector ke saath asteroids ki descent speed aur spawn rate increase hoti hai.
  • Word Difficulty Progression: Sector 1 me short (3-4 letter) words aate hain, jabki Sector 3 aur upar me complex multi-syllable cosmic vocabulary deploy hoti hai.
  • Live Typing Analytics: Real-time HUD bar continuous taur par aapka WPM (Words Per Minute) aur typing Accuracy benchmark track karta hai.
🛡️ Critical Survival Tip: Hamesha bottom line ke sabse nazdeek wale asteroid par focus karein! Top par naye spawn hone wale words se distract na hon. Home-row tactile bumps (F aur J) par ungliyan rakh kar blind typing practice karein taaki reaction time zero ho sake.
