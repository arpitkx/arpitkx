How To Play Falling Words

Master sky defense rules, laser lock-on mechanics, and shield preservation strategies.

01

Acquire & Lock Target

Aasman se neeche aane wale kisi bhi word ka pehla letter type karein. Gun turret turant us word par lock ho jayegi aur cyan tracer lines fire karegi.

02

Laser Vaporization Sequence

Targeted word ke baaki letters sequence me accurate type karein. Har right character par green illumination aayegi aur word complete hote hi laser blast se explode hoga.

03

Unlock / Emergency Retarget

Agar aapne lambe word par lock kar liya hai aur doosra word shield barrier ke paas aa gaya hai, toh Backspace dabakar target cancel karein aur dangerous word ko pehle shoot karein.

04

Shield Perimeter (3 Lives)

Aapke paas total 3 Shields (Lives) hain. Jab bhi koi word dotted red barrier line ko touch karega, 1 shield destroy hogi aur combo streak reset ho jayegi.

⚡ Wave Escalation & Multiplier Rules

  • Combo Multiplier: Lagataar 5 bina error ke letters type karne par combo boost milta hai (up to 4x Multiplier). Ek bhi galat key press streak ko tod deti hai.
  • Score Formula: Har destroyed word par score milta hai: Points = Word Length × 15 × Wave Level × Multiplier.
  • Wave Progression: Har 8 words destroy karne par agla wave start hota hai, jisme falling speed aur spawn rate badh jaati hai.
  • Vocabulary Pool: Waves aage badhne ke saath 4-letter short words se transition hokar complex sci-fi terms aane lagte hain.
🛡️ Critical Survival Tip: Hamesha shield line ke kareeb aane wale words ko pehle target karein! Keyboard ki jagah seedhe screen par dekhte hue touch-typing home row (F aur J) discipline follow karein.
